# Evidence-led video production

The editor can start from a finished shot or from a paper and follow the same work in either direction.

**Query receipt** records a specific research question, answer or retrieval instance and its cited sources. An original paper and a query receipt answer different questions.

**Source** identifies the original publication, the relevant passage or figure, and who checked its relevance.

**Claim** states one fact the editor intends to communicate, together with the limits of the supporting material.

**Concept** records a creative decision such as a fixed camera position. It may constrain multiple prompts without implying scientific support.

**Prompt revision** records the submitted wording, the creative rationale and the exact claims it depends on.

**Draft** is an inspectable depiction whose inputs, rendering method and file can be recovered. Its data basis identifies whether it uses measured, published or illustrative values.

**Generation intent** names one requested operation, including the input draft and settings. Its receipt associates the same request with what the provider actually accepted and returned.

**Asset** is a reusable production object with a version, origin and usage rights. A shot selects a particular version and a time interval.

**Needs review** means an input revision or its interpretation has changed. The former version remains available to inspect.
